Ms. Lola Oshin

About Founder

Empowering the next generation through learning

Lola Oshin MCCT, an educator, therapist, counsellor, and advocate for children's emotional well-being. She has dedicated herself to understanding not only how children and adults learn, but how they feel, grow, heal, and flourish. Her work is deeply rooted in the belief that every child and every adult deserves to be seen, heard, valued, and given the opportunity to thrive regardless of their starting point.

As a counselor and therapeutic practitioner, Ms. Lola is particularly passionate about emotional healing and personal growth. She recognizes the silent battles many people face and seeks to create tools, resources, and experiences that promote self-awareness, emotional regulation, hope, and healing. Her vision extends beyond the classroom into the wider community, where she strives to support both children and adults in living healthier and more purposeful lives.

At the center of everything she does is a simple but powerful conviction: every individual carries unique gifts, and when those gifts are nurtured with patience, understanding, and encouragement, extraordinary things can happen. Lola's story is one of service, compassion, faith, resilience, and an enduring commitment to helping others become the very best version of themselves.

Her therapeutic approach is integrative and client-centered, drawing from modalities such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), and mindfulness-based practices. She is deeply committed to helping clients develop emotional resilience, build healthier relationships, and reconnect with their strengths.

In addition to her clinical work, Lola regularly attends continuing education trainings and mental health conferences to stay informed on the latest therapeutic tools and best practices.
