Ms Lola Oshin

Licensed Mental Health Therapist · Transformative MindsNg

Lola Oshin MCCT, an educator, therapist, counsellor, and advocate for children's emotional well-being. She has dedicated herself to understanding not only how children and adults learn, but how they feel, grow, heal, and flourish. Her work is deeply rooted in the belief that every child and every adult deserves to be seen, heard, valued, and given the opportunity to thrive regardless of their starting point.

As a counselor and therapeutic practitioner, Ms. Lola is particularly passionate about emotional healing and personal growth. She recognizes the silent battles many people face and seeks to create tools, resources, and experiences that promote self-awareness, emotional regulation, hope, and healing. Her vision extends beyond the classroom into the wider community, where she strives to support both children and adults in living healthier and more purposeful lives.

At the center of everything she does is a simple but powerful conviction: every individual carries unique gifts, and when those gifts are nurtured with patience, understanding, and encouragement, extraordinary things can happen. Lola's story is one of service, compassion, faith, resilience, and an enduring commitment to helping others become the very best version of themselves. In addition to her clinical work, Lola regularly attends continuing education trainings and mental health conferences to stay informed on the latest therapeutic tools and best practices.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Have more questions? Reach out to us directly.

How do I know if I need therapy? +
If you find yourself feeling persistently overwhelmed, sad, anxious, or struggling to function in daily life, therapy can be a powerful support. You don't have to be in crisis — many people cometo therapy simply to grow, gain clarity, and improvetheir well-being.
Do you offer virtual / online sessions? +
Yes! We offer flexible teletherapy sessions via video call or phone, making it convenient to access care from anywhere in Nigeria. In-person sessions are also available.
Is everything I share kept confidential? +
Absolutely. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of therapy. Everything you share with your therapist is private, with very few exceptions required by law (such as immediate risk of harm). You can speak freely knowing you are safe.
How long does a therapy session last? +
Each session typically lasts 50–60 minutes. The number of sessions you need depends on your goals and circumstances —your therapist will work with you to create a personalized plan.
What makes Transformative MindsNg different? +
We are rooted in compassion, faith, and a deeply non judgemental approach. Guided by Galatians 6:2, we believe in bearing burdens together. Our therapists understand the unique cultural context of Nigeria and provide care that is both evidence-based and culturally sensitive.
